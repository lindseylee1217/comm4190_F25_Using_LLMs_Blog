Now that I’ve played around with the gpt-5 model to generate fun thumbnail images with Miffy, I am interested in using other image-generation models to see how they compare. In this post, I will be comparing gpt-5, ImagineArt 1.0, and Gemini 2.5 Flash Image.

I generated the prompt to feed to these various models with gpt-5, using the same format as I did in the previous blog. The prompt is as follows:

Please write me an image prompt that would be generate an image including two elements:

  • the dutch character miffy

  • anything relating to [blog post content]

I then revised the output image prompt to ensure that it includes the following statement: “This image should be appropriate for a blog post thumbnail and should be a square in size.”

Reflection

Overall, I found that the images generated by gpt-5 and gemini were the most appropriate for my blog posts and fitting in my vision. ImageineArt generations were very detailed and high-quality, however, they lacked consistency in its generation and did not accurately illustrate the character Miffy, likely due to copyright concerns in its training data.

In contrast, the gpt-5 and gemini models accurately illustrated Miffy, though in different manners. The gemini model leaned towards depicting Miffy in blues, and more cool-toned colors. It also made use of thinner lines than the gpt-5 model. The gpt-5 model had a distinct style when illustrating Miffy, using many warm colors and yellows in the image generation.

Now that I’ve compared all three models, I am happy to have finished this exploration as now I prefer the images the gemini model generated. Not only did I prefer the quality of image generation, but the images also generated more quickly and greater detail.
